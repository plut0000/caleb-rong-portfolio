SignSpeak

Sign in ASL. Read English. Hear it spoken.

Someone signs on camera, a 200-sign model reads the signs, and the browser speaks the English translation aloud. It reaches 90.3% top-1 accuracy on the test set. Built for DECA's Entrepreneurship Innovation Plan.

Built with Next.js, MediaPipe, ONNX Runtime and Gemini

Locus

Where was this photo taken?

Drop in a photo and get a best-guess location on a map. Locus uses the photo's GPS when it has it, and when it has to guess from what's in the picture, it draws a 10 to 30 km circle instead of a falsely precise pin.

Built with React, Leaflet, exifr and OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini

Cognify

Turn class notes into study sessions.

Upload your notes and Cognify builds summaries, flashcards, quizzes, slideshows and a study coach, all from your own material. It's open source under the MIT license.

Built with Next.js, PDF.js, Gemini and Auth.js

CallCheck

Find out how your phone is answered when you're not there.

An AI caller phones a business as an ordinary customer, then grades how fast staff picked up, how they greeted, whether the facts were right and whether they offered a time.

Built with Next.js, Vapi, OpenAI and Vercel Blob

Every project shows its work

None of them hand back an answer without saying where it came from or how sure it is.

SignSpeak
Accepts a sign only when its confidence checks pass, and labels each translation with the path that produced it: the dedicated model or Gemini video.
Locus
Sizes its circle to how sure it is: 1.7 km when the photo has GPS, 10 to 30 km for a visual guess, with a short rationale on the map.
Cognify
Builds every summary, flashcard, quiz question and coach answer from the notebook you uploaded.
CallCheck
Grades the call against the facts you entered, with a separate score for each of four areas and the fixes that matter most.
