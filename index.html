Locus

Estimates where a photo was taken.

Drop in a photo and Locus places it on a map. It reads embedded GPS data when there is some, falls back to a vision model when there isn't, and shows an uncertainty radius instead of pretending to be exact.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Leaflet
  • Express

About

I like starting with one question: could this be a little easier?

These projects answer it in different ways: helping people communicate across ASL and English, finding the story behind a photo, and turning a pile of notes into something you can study from.

I care about tools that are honest about their limits. SignSpeak shows how confident it is, and Locus draws a radius instead of claiming an exact spot. All of the code is public if you want to look under the hood.
