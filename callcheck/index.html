CallCheck

Find out how your phone is answered when you're not there.

CallCheck is a robot mystery shopper for a business phone line. It calls as an ordinary customer, then grades the call and coaches the team on how fast they picked up, how they greeted, whether the facts were right and whether they offered a time.

Built for any front desk.

Ready-made setups for dealerships, salons, restaurants, clinics and home services, plus a custom mode for anything else.

How it works.

Set up the facts, let an AI caller ring the business, and get a scorecard when it hangs up.

Step 1

Set up the call.

Pick an industry and a scenario, like booking a service appointment, then enter what staff should say: hours, prices and current offers.

Step 2

Let the shopper call.

A Vapi voice assistant phones the business as a regular customer, or staff call it from the business line. The call is recorded and transcribed.

Step 3

Get the scorecard.

The transcript is graded against the facts you entered, with a letter grade, the top fixes and a line staff can practise for each one.

From the call to the coaching.

Watch the transcript come in as the call happens, then see exactly what to fix.

Under the hood.

One config per industry.

Each industry defines its fact fields, scenarios, caller persona, grading rubric and coaching lines. The demo scripts and the scorer are generated from the same config.

Two ways to grade.

An OpenAI model scores each call against the industry's rubric. Without a key, a deterministic rule-based scorer checks the same four areas.

Live transcripts.

Vapi sends call events to an authenticated webhook that keeps each call's transcript in private Vercel Blob storage, so the page updates every second.

Consent built in.

Every test needs a consent checkbox, real calls need an access code, and the server refuses a live call without consent. Public demo scoring is rate-limited.
